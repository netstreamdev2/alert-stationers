# Ink & Highlighter

Even ink strokes keep the UI clear, while 10° magenta swipes translate the digital logo’s energy into stationery marks. The tapered refresh and two-arrow symbol retain the strongest family cue. This is strongest in dense shop and admin interfaces. Its risk is that the swipes can feel generic unless their angle and placement stay disciplined.

Draft exploration. Palette and arrow geometry require the master SVG. All UI is upright; 1.75px strokes with round joins. Refresh uses filled tapered crescents as a deliberate logo-motif exception within the outline set.
