# Paper Cut

Bevelled UI joins and layered, cut-out spot silhouettes echo the logo’s blunt arrowheads and solid mass. Raspberry dominates the larger paper forms, connecting the exploration to the store sign. This is strongest in category browsing and welcoming illustrations. Its risk is heavier small-size silhouettes, especially at 16px.

Draft exploration. Palette and arrow geometry require the master SVG. All UI is upright; 2px strokes with bevel joins. Refresh uses filled tapered crescents as a deliberate logo-motif exception within the outline set.
